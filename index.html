Things I've Done

I have been many things in my careeer to date, including IT Technician, Software Trainer, Language Teacher (in the UK and abroad), Senior Lecturer, Personal Tutor, Lead Researcher, University Programme Director, UX/UI Developer, Exam Assessor and Moderator, Learning Resource Developer, and Learning & Development Consultant.

A few of my more recent achievements (both in-work and out-of-work) include:

My Academic Monograph

After gaining my PhD, part of my post-doctoral work investigated the function and description of British Sign Language, leading to the publication of this book under Bloomsbury Academic. I completed this under the linguistic framework of Systemic Functional Linguistics: a framework that I still employ to this day in my current role.

Developing, Launching, and Evaluating the Impact of Training on a National Scale

In my current role, I led an organisation-wide project to design, develop, and launch a set of training materials for all colleagues. This included the full Training Needs Analysis phrase, and the subsequent evaluation, impact, and continuous improvement stages.

Volunteering Digital Production Services to Independent Collectives

As part of the TownQueer Games Bundle, I volunteered with the organising committee to create the trailer for the bundle's launch. I used various compositing and motion graphics software packages to complete this work, which would go on to raise over USD$6,290 (roughly £4,700) for LGBTQ+ game developers.

Generative Plotter Art via JavaScript

I have recently started to use JavaScript to create generative pathways and patterns, which I then export to various formats for further editing and plotting on an XY plotter. I currently use a slightly modified Cricut Explore 5 for this, but hope to upgrade to a more advanced plotter in the future.
